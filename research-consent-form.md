## What this study involves
You are being invited to take part in a short research study on QR-code phishing awareness. At some point during the study window (the next [X] days), you may encounter a **simulated phishing scenario** — for example, a QR code linking to a realistic-looking but entirely fake webpage. You will not be told the exact time or form this will take in advance, because that foreknowledge would defeat the purpose of the study.

## What we collect
- Whether you scanned/opened the simulated page
- Whether you started filling in or submitted the form on that page
- How long you took to decide
- **We do not read, transmit, or store anything you type into any form field.** Only the fact that a submission happened is recorded.
- No name, email, or student ID is linked to your responses — you are represented only by a random, anonymous ID.

## Your rights
- Taking part is entirely voluntary.
- You may withdraw at any time, for any reason, without penalty.
- Immediately after any interaction with the simulated page, you will see a debrief screen explaining what happened and why.
- You may request that your data be deleted at any point by contacting [your email].

## Data use
Data is used only for this academic research project and will be reported in aggregate (e.g., "42% of participants in Condition B submitted the form"), never individually.
